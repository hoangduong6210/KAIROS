# KAIROS Research Wiki

This wiki owns the living scientific interpretation of KAIROS. Code,
datasets, result artifacts, and paper files remain in their specialized
directories; the wiki records what they establish and whether wording is
eligible for publication.

Start with [START-HERE](START-HERE.md). Scientific reviewers should continue
to the [claim registry](claims/Current-Claim-Language.md), [evidence ledger](evidence/Evidence-Ledger.md),
and [limitations](LIMITATIONS.md). The [index](INDEX.md) lists every maintained
page and its semantic owner.

The [project knowledge map](PROJECT-KNOWLEDGE-MAP.md) connects every repository
surface to a wiki owner. The [traceability policy](evidence/Traceability-Policy.md)
defines the mandatory claim-to-evidence chain, and the wiki audit runs with the
repository tests so documentation drift fails mechanically rather than becoming
silent.

Artifact completion is not claim admission. `paper_source: true` is allowed
only for reviewed prose backed by admitted claim IDs. No current page has that
status while benchmark-v1 awaits publication admission and `paper/CURRENT`
remains `UNRELEASED`.
