# Research Questions

## RQ-REGIME-001

Can a state-constrained temporal graph representation improve a predeclared
regime-transition endpoint, relative to matched strong temporal baselines,
under leakage-resistant time splits and repeated seeds while providing stable
model-internal attribution?

The primary endpoint, target population, uncertainty unit, intervention policy,
lead-time definition, and acceptance gate remain to be frozen. “Causal” cannot
be used as an identification conclusion without a separate causal estimand and design.

## RQ-EARLY-001

Can a preregistered model-derived warning score improve a frozen lead-time
estimand without using future information in fitting, thresholding, smoothing,
or event selection?

## RQ-XDOMAIN-001

Does a frozen representation and training protocol transfer to a separately
declared domain under matched baselines, repeated seeds, complete failure
accounting, and domain-appropriate external-validity review?

## RQ-CAUSAL-001

What causal estimand, assumptions, intervention data, identification argument,
and falsification tests would be required before any KAIROS output could support
causal rather than structural/model-internal wording?
